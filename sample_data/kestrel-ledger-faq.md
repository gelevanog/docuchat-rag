# Kestrel Ledger Product FAQ

Kestrel Ledger is a fictional online bookkeeping application for freelancers and small businesses, made by Kestrel Grove Ltd. This FAQ is demo content for DocuChat.

## Plans and Pricing

### What plans are available?

Kestrel Ledger has three plans. **Solo** costs 9 EUR per month and includes one user, unlimited invoices and one connected bank account. **Team** costs 29 EUR per month and includes up to five users, five bank connections and multi-currency invoicing. **Business** costs 79 EUR per month and adds unlimited users, approval workflows, single sign-on and priority support.

Paying annually gives a 20% discount on every plan.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace starts with a 30-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is required to start the trial. When the trial ends, the workspace becomes read-only until a plan is chosen; no data is deleted.

### Can I get a refund?

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and are not refunded for the current month. Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, we refund the unused months minus the annual discount.

## Banking and Integrations

### Which banks can I connect?

Kestrel Ledger connects to more than 2,500 banks in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom through licensed open-banking providers. Transactions are synchronised four times per day. You can also import statements in CSV, OFX or CAMT.053 format.

### Which apps does Kestrel Ledger integrate with?

Native integrations are available for Stripe, PayPal, Shopify and Google Drive. The Business plan also includes access to the REST API and outgoing webhooks, limited to 600 API requests per minute per workspace.

### Does it support multiple currencies?

The Team and Business plans support invoicing in 38 currencies. Exchange rates are fetched daily from the European Central Bank reference rates, and you can override the rate on individual invoices.

## Security and Privacy

### Where is my data stored?

All customer data is stored in data centres in Frankfurt, Germany, with an encrypted backup copy in Dublin, Ireland. Data never leaves the European Union.

### How is my data protected?

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Backups are taken every hour and kept for 35 days. Kestrel Grove holds an ISO 27001 certification, and an independent penetration test is performed every year.

### Do you support two-factor authentication and SSO?

Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app or a hardware security key is available on every plan, and workspace owners can make it mandatory. Single sign-on through SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect is included in the Business plan.

### What happens to my data if I close my account?

When you close your workspace, you can export everything first. The data is then kept for 90 days in case you change your mind, after which it is permanently deleted from production systems. Backups containing the data expire within a further 35 days.

## Data Export

### Can I export my data?

Yes, at any time and on every plan. You can export invoices as PDF, and all transactions, contacts and ledger entries as CSV or as a SAF-T file for your accountant. A full workspace export is prepared in the background and a download link is emailed to you; the link stays valid for seven days.

## Support

### How do I contact support?

Support is available by email and in-app chat from Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time. The typical first response time is under four hours. Business plan customers get priority support with a first response within one hour and a dedicated account manager.

### Is there a status page?

Yes. Service availability and incident reports are published on the public status page. Our uptime commitment for the Business plan is 99.9% per calendar month; if we miss it, affected customers receive a service credit of 10% of that month's fee.
