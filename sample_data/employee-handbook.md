# Kestrel Grove Employee Handbook

This handbook summarises the working policies of Kestrel Grove Ltd., a fictional software company used as demo content for DocuChat. It applies to all permanent employees. Contractors should refer to their individual agreements.

## Working Hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside of core hours you may organise your day as you see fit, provided your weekly total is around 38 hours. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours unless every attendee has agreed in advance.

Overtime is not expected as a normal part of the job. If a release or incident requires more than 5 hours of overtime in a single week, your manager must approve it in writing, and the extra hours can be taken back as time off within the following two months.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Employees may work fully remotely from any country where Kestrel Grove has a registered entity: the United Kingdom, Portugal and Poland. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year requires approval from the People team, because it can create tax obligations for both you and the company.

Hybrid employees attached to the Lisbon or Kraków office are asked to be on site on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Desks are booked through the office app no later than 18:00 on the previous day.

Each remote employee receives a one-time home office allowance of 600 EUR for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment, plus a monthly internet stipend of 30 EUR paid with salary.

## Paid Time Off

### Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to the public holidays of their country of employment. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount. After three full years of service, the allowance increases to 28 days.

Vacation requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

### Sick Leave

If you are ill, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. You do not need a doctor's note for absences of up to three consecutive working days. From the fourth day, please upload a medical certificate to the HR portal. Sick days never reduce your vacation allowance.

### Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents, including adoptive parents, receive 10 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 12 months after the child's arrival. Statutory benefits are topped up by the company to 100% of base salary.

### Volunteering Day

Every employee may take one paid volunteering day per year to support a registered charity or community project of their choice.

## Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted through the expense tool within 30 days, with a photo of the receipt. Expenses under 50 EUR are approved automatically; anything above that requires approval from your manager.

Travel should be booked through the company travel desk. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours; for longer flights, premium economy may be booked. Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 150 EUR per night, or 220 EUR per night in London.

Meals while travelling are covered up to a daily limit of 60 EUR. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client events approved by a director.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 EUR that can be used for courses, books, conference tickets and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over. Up to five working days per year can be used for learning activities without taking vacation.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from at least two peers, and a conversation with your manager. Salary adjustments are decided once a year, following the April review, and take effect from 1 June.

## Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every three years, or sooner if the device fails. Company equipment must be returned within ten working days after your last day of employment.

## Leaving the Company

The notice period is one month during the six-month probation period and three months afterwards, unless your contract states otherwise. Remaining vacation days are paid out with the final salary.
