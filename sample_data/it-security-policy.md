# Kestrel Grove IT Security Policy

This policy describes the security rules that every Kestrel Grove employee and contractor must follow. Kestrel Grove is a fictional company; this document is demo content for DocuChat.

## Accounts and Passwords

Every person receives a personal account. Sharing accounts or passwords, including with colleagues, is not allowed.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be stored in the company password manager. Reusing a company password on any other website is forbidden. Passwords do not expire on a schedule, but must be changed immediately if there is any suspicion that they have been exposed.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source code hosting, cloud consoles and the HR portal. Hardware security keys are issued to engineers with production access; everyone else may use an authenticator app. SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor.

## Devices

Only company-managed laptops may be used to access customer data or production systems. Managed laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled, automatic operating system updates turned on, and the screen must lock after five minutes of inactivity.

Personal phones may be used for email and chat only after enrolling them in the mobile device management app, which enforces a screen lock and allows the company data on the phone to be wiped remotely.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT helpdesk within one hour of noticing the loss, so it can be locked and wiped.

## Data Classification

Company information is classified into four levels:

- **Public**: information intended for anyone, such as marketing pages and published documentation.
- **Internal**: day-to-day information for employees, such as meeting notes and internal wiki pages.
- **Confidential**: customer data, financial reports, contracts and employee records. Access is granted on a need-to-know basis.
- **Restricted**: encryption keys, production credentials and security incident details. Access requires approval from the security team and is reviewed every quarter.

Confidential and restricted information must never be copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage or public AI tools. Approved AI assistants are listed on the internal wiki, and customer data may only be used with those assistants that are covered by a data processing agreement.

## Access Reviews

Managers review the access rights of their team members every six months. When someone changes role, access that is no longer required must be removed within five working days. When someone leaves the company, all accounts are disabled on their last working day before 18:00.

## Reporting Security Incidents

A security incident is any event that may affect the confidentiality, integrity or availability of company or customer data, for example a phishing email you clicked, a misdirected email containing customer data, or malware on a laptop.

Report every suspected incident immediately in the #security-incidents channel or by calling the security on-call phone. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete evidence. It is always better to report something that turns out to be harmless.

The security team classifies incidents within two hours. If personal data is affected, the data protection officer is informed so that the supervisory authority can be notified within 72 hours where the law requires it.

## Phishing Simulations

The security team runs a phishing simulation every quarter. Employees who click the simulated link are asked to complete a short training module; nobody is penalised for failing a simulation.

## Working While Travelling

When working from public places, use the company VPN and a privacy screen filter. Never leave your laptop unattended in a public place or in a car. Travel to countries on the internal high-risk list requires a loaner laptop issued by IT at least five working days before departure.

## Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested through the IT helpdesk, approved by the head of security, and are valid for a maximum of six months.
